Try-on bench

One job, isolated from the app: put the garment from an image onto this mannequin and check the result honestly. Nothing here touches the wardrobe, the stylist or the closet. The point of the bench is the validation panel — a generated try-on is only usable if the mannequin came back unaltered, and that is measurable rather than a matter of opinion.
